# Product — Zahrat Al Hayat (مركز زهرة الحياة)

**Register:** brand (the public site is the product); the dashboard is the product register.

## What it is
A human-development, wellness and healing training center in Sweida, Syria, founded and led by Dr. Reem Al Hayek. It offers specialized courses, applied workshops, individual and group sessions (meditation, breathwork, hypnotherapy) and therapeutic yoga. The site lets visitors learn about the center and request a consultation.

## Users
- **Visitors / prospective clients** — Arabic-first (RTL), seeking calm, self-awareness and healing. Often unfamiliar with specific modalities, so warmth and reassurance matter more than jargon.
- **Admin / teachers** — the founder and coaches manage content, gallery, teachers, appointments and users from the dashboard. They embody the brand, so the dashboard must feel like the same product as the public site.

## Tone & brand
Serene, warm, trustworthy, premium-but-human. Never clinical, corporate, or SaaS-generic. Credibility comes from the founder and the work, never from fabricated metrics or stock social proof.

## Languages
Bilingual Arabic (default, RTL) + English (LTR). All UI must preserve logical `start`/`end` direction and per-direction typography. Dates use `ar-SY` / `en-US`.

## Strategic principles
- Lead with the founder and the real practice, not templates.
- One tactile signature (botanical line-art) instead of decorative gradients/blur.
- Honesty over trust theater: no invented ratings, avatars, or numbers.
- Payments are not enabled yet; bookings complete via a WhatsApp handoff.

## Anti-references
Generic SaaS dashboards, gradient-text hero cards, identical icon-card grids, fabricated "hundreds of happy clients + 5 stars" widgets, rainbow admin palettes.
